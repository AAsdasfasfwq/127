# S2 · 138.20 → 302.25 · "The Boulder" — visual plan

Owner: S2 agent. Code: `src/sections/s2/`. This is the film's first action climax: the visual
space literally **narrows** in this section — open warm narrows and a wide blue slit at the start,
a vertical stone shaft and a thin cold sliver of sky at the end.

## 0. Three decisions a template would never make (atelier rule)

* **Structural — the inversion match.** The canyon is read twice as the same shape with opposite
  luminance: on the map it is a *dark crack in bright rock*; from inside it is a *bright crack of
  sky in dark rock*. We cut between the two at 145.3 (map → inside) and again, reversed, at 253.4
  (shaft → map) — the section is bookended by its own motif. After the crash every frame is
  composed around a **vertical slot** (rock masses crowd the frame edges, `slotFrame`), and the
  slot only opens again when the "engineering mind takes over" (264.5).
* **Colour — blue drains.** Sky blue is wide and saturated in the narrows (outside world near),
  absent during the fall, a thin distant sliver over the trapped man, and in the inventory the
  **only blue is the drinking tube** on the pack (the future tourniquet). Red appears first as the
  freeze-frame physics ink (178), owns the fall (danger) and the pressure zone, then disappears into
  slate `shadow` (243+) — and comes back once, as the sardonic strike through LEATHERMAN.
* **Type — type with mass.** Numbers in this section *weigh something*: `800 LB` falls, lands, cracks
  the frame and kicks dust; the HOUR counter is born huge in the dust cloud and docks into the corner;
  LEATHERMAN gets struck through. Everything else is small mono labels on diagrams.

## 3. Palette, type, motifs

* Palettes: `canyon` (138–177) → print + red ink (178–184) → `canyon` dimmed (184–198) → `danger`
  (198–227) → dust ochre → `shadow` slate (243–276) → slate `#2E3A48` + warm top light (276–302).
* Type: Big Shoulders (APR 26, 2003, 800 LB, < 3 FT, 12 FL OZ, INVENTORY, LEATHERMAN), Martian Mono
  (labels, HOUR, map, catalogue numbers), Instrument Serif italic (grim, not even a genuine),
  no other families. Text never repeats the sentence being spoken.
* Motifs: **chockstone** = `drawChockstone` everywhere in 2D (scar + lichen eye + bands); the 3D
  mesh uses the same ramp, bands, cap, scar and lichen eye. **HOUR** born at 212.5 (`HOURS.trapped`),
  on screen 212.5–227 and again 261–276 (`0.5` = inventory). **Slit** = the sky in every in-slot shot,
  narrowing. **Strata** = map contours, geology, wipe. **Red dust** = the crash, the settling shaft.
* Aron: Saturday — `beard 0.05`, `dirt` 0 → 0.35 after the fall, `tired` 0 → 0.2, sunburn 0.45.
