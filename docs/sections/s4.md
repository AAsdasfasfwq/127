# S4 · 454.20 → 600.76 · "The Diary & The Vision" — visual plan

Owner: S4 agent. Code: `src/sections/s4/`. This is the plan written before building; the
"as built" notes at the end record deviations.

## 0. Intent

The camcorder he brought for bike stunts becomes the only witness of a man saying goodbye.
Then the body fails, the canyon turns on him (delirium), he carves his own epitaph — and in
the darkest hour the film's single burst of gold and green arrives: a son who does not exist
yet. The jolt of that vision is what sends him into S5.

Emotional curve: intimate → accepting → tender goodbye → clinical decay → genuinely
unsettling → funereal → hushed night → **the most beautiful minute** → electric jolt →
cold, clear resolve.

### Three decisions a template would never make (atelier rule)
* **Structure — aspect ratio as story.** The diary lives in the camcorder's own **4:3 DV
  frame** (soft pillarbox, OSD, tape noise); reality is 16:9; the vision alone opens to
  **2.39:1 CinemaScope** bars that slide in with the light. Three ways of seeing.
* **Colour — gold is rationed to one minute.** Everything before the vision is DV green-slate,
  violet delirium or cold stone. Gold and the film's only lush green appear at 559.4 and are
  gone at 588.2 — except one tiny golden reflection of the boy in Aron's iris in the last shot.
* **Type — the vision has no text at all.** The only stretch of the film with zero type.
  Everywhere else type is tactile and diegetic: camcorder OSD (Martian Mono), handwriting in
  blue ink (Reenie Beanie), letters scratched into the wall (Rock Salt, raking light).

### Motifs used
* **The camcorder** (3D hero, then sprite, then UI) = the confessor. Its red tally dot is the
  first red; it becomes a red blood cell (shape match).
* **Sky-blue threads** (S1 callback): the goodbye messages travel up them — one-way, frayed.
* **The blank note** (S1 callback) on the kitchen table: "never telling them where he was going".
* **The slit of sky**: as a survival gauge (closing to a hairline at "zero"), as the night
  between WED and THU, as the pre-dawn blue catchlight in his eye at the end (B4).
* **Strata lines**: cross-section pull-back into anonymity.
* **The chockstone**: always present in the trapped shots (canonical `drawChockstone`).
* **HOUR counter**: 69 → 93 at WEDNESDAY (502.6), 105 on the vision night (547.6).
* Aron continuity (Wed): beard 0.8, dirt 0.7, tired 0.75, sunburn 0.35.

## 2. Graphics mix (planned screen time)
* 3D ≈ 22 s (15 %): camcorder hero, blood cells, can, carving, epitaph reprise.
* 2.5D ≈ 59 s (40 %): confessor, REC macro, LCD pull-back, threads, kitchen, hallucination,
  knife, pull-back, light seed, the vision.
* 2D ≈ 65 s (45 %): gauge, viewfinder + type, sorry, instructions, Wednesday, lips, slip,
  voices, faces/eyes, head silhouette, ECG.
* Motion design (UI, infographic, kinetic type, diagrams, typographic 3D) ≈ 60 %; character
  animation concentrated in the confessor, kitchen, delirium, vision, eyes.

## 5. Vision design sheet (for S7's "Leo" callback)
Reusable drawing functions are exported from `src/sections/s4/vision_world.js`

  cowlick), round face, rosy cheeks, yellow tee, olive shorts, tiny red sneakers (mini-Aron).

  sleeves, dark jeans, matte-graphite prosthetic right forearm with a steel wrist ring.
