# S1 · 0.00 → 138.20 · "The Maze & The Man" — visual plan

Arc: **majestic → confident swagger → ironic warning → isolation → into the crack.**
S1 is the film's promise: the open, warm world that the rest of the film will take away.

## Three decisions a template would never make (atelier rule)

1. **Structure — the frame narrows.** Every act of S1 ends tighter than it began: horizon-wide 3D
   flight → top-down maze → a single carved wall → a man → an office cubicle → a blank card →
   a road that shrinks to a dot → a 20-inch crack. The last image is a vertical slot composition
   (two walls, one strip of sky) that S2 inherits.
2. **Colour — blue is rationed and then taken away.** Sky blue `#5FB8E6` appears only as the sky
   and as the threads that tie Aron to people (parents, friends, roommates, phone signal). From
   101.6 s the threads snap one by one; when the last one snaps on "help" (111.3 s) the frame
   holds **no blue at all** until the thin distant sky in the slot (≥ 126.7 s).
3. **Type — the land speaks in stone, the man speaks in drafting.** The wilderness's words are
   *cut into rock* (chiselled MISTAKES / CARELESSNESS / OVERCONFIDENCE, the year 2003 standing as
   sandstone monoliths). Aron's self-image is *engineering drafting* (PROFILE chart, spec sheet,
   title block, Martian Mono callouts) and **INVULNERABLE** as forged steel plates.
