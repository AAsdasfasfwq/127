# S3 · 302.25 → 454.20 · "Plans, Night, Thirst" — visual plan

Owner: S3 agent. Code: `src/sections/s3/`. Arc: **methodical hope → futility → engineering hope →
collapse → dread → cold → thirst → fifteen minutes of warmth → cold again.**

## 0. The three decisions a template would never make (atelier rule)

| Decision | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Structural** | The plans live on **physical drafting sheets** (Sheet A, Sheet B) that the film keeps returning to; reality (macro canyon shots) keeps interrupting the drawings; each failed plan is **struck through with a single red ruled line** and the sheet is physically lifted away. The **rope** is the section's through-line object: Plan B tool → night cocoon → the fibres of a mind that unravels. | An engineer's mind made visible; the rope carries meaning across three beats. |
| **Colour** | The drafting world is a **Van Dyke brownprint** (bone lines on oxide-brown paper), *not* a blueprint — because in this film **blue = other people / help**. Sky blue appears only twice here: as the far-away search that arrives too late (timeline) and in the distant sky slit. Gold appears only for the 15 minutes of sun. Red only for removal zones, failures, pain. | Colour is earned and keeps its meaning. |
| **Type** | Plan titles as an editorial **richLine**: Fraunces italic *Plan* + Big Shoulders 900 **A/B** + Martian Mono tracked subtitle. Drafting annotations in Martian Mono; key numbers in Big Shoulders (`660 LB`, `0.000 IN`, `15 MIN`). The word MIND is the only type in the film that *breaks* (slices/jitters) as it unravels. | One voice, one controlled break. |

Drafting vocabulary used precisely: **phantom lines** (long-short-short dashes) for Aron's body and
for the original straight blade, **section hatching** for rock, **dimension lines** with ticks,
**detail callout circles** ("DETAIL B · 40:1"), **title blocks**, an **exploded view** with part
numbers, **force vectors** and **ΣF = 0** (the static-equilibrium condition — the engineer's irony:
the rock is in perfect equilibrium).
