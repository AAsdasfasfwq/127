# S6 · 717.05 → 885.26 · "The Hardest Part & The Walk Out" — visual plan

Owner: S6 agent. Code: `src/sections/s6/`. Arc: unbearable intensity → white-hot nerve → **FREE (HOUR 127)**
→ alive but dying → the vertical drop → the burning march → voices, people, colour.

## 1. Three decisions a template would never make

* **Structure — "one line, then the rings"**: Part I (the surgery) is built from exactly two graphic
  devices: **concentric rings** (the forearm cross-section read as sandstone strata seen end-on) and
  **one line** that keeps changing identity — incision line → blade edge → tendon cord → nerve cable →
  the fuse to the brain → lightning → the tension line that snaps at "pull" → the vertical gap of light
  between Aron and the boulder → the sky slit that tears open. After freedom the same line becomes the
  rope, the route, the footprint trail, the sound wave of the voices and finally the phone's searching
  signal (handed to S7, where the S1 blue threads reconnect).
* **Colour — no blue until he is free**: 717–782.9 is only near-black `#120D0B`, signal red, bone and
  white-hot (+ Aron's skin). Sky blue and gold flood in for the first time at 782.94 through the torn
  slit. Then warm canyon → `PAL.bleach` (colour literally drained by the sun) → sky blue returns with the
  voices and the Meijer family; the water bottle is the bluest object in the section.
* **Type — clinical mono, three numbers in display**: during the surgery all labels are small Martian
  Mono; display type is rationed to the numbers that matter — `2 IN`, `1:05:00`, **`127`** (the canonical
  HOUR counter at 5× scale, flying from the HUD corner to frame centre). After: `−25 %`, `65 FT`, `≈ 6 MI`.
  Human words are Instrument Serif italic ("the hardest part"); the rasped request is handwriting.

**The one "wow"** (782.94): the HUD hour counter in the corner rolls 126→127 *as* the canyon walls
split along the sky slit — the counter flies to the centre and grows into a 560-px `127` standing in a
flood of gold/sky light while the walls fly out of frame with parallax.

No gore anywhere: schematics (rings, cords, cable, body silhouettes), light, silhouette, the face, sound.
