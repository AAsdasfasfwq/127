# S5 · 600.76 → 717.05 · "The Decision" — visual plan

**Arc:** cold resolve → why it seemed impossible → the engineering insight → monumental
statement → preparation → the two breaks. **Tone:** brutal, precise, restrained. No gore, no
wounds: the breaks are told through radiographs, schematics, silhouette, light and sound.

## The three decisions a template would never make
* **Structure — one object, many lenses.** The hero of S5 is a single object: Aron's pinned right
  forearm. Every beat shows *the same arm* through a different lens — a cold real close-up, a
  19th-century anatomical engraving, an engraved cross-section, a 3D x-ray lever, an x-ray
  cross-section on an engineering sheet, a top-down prep flat-lay, a 3D x-ray with a tourniquet,
  a silhouette, a radiograph. The lens changes with the meaning; the arm stays.
* **Colour — the turn from paper to dark.** "Why it was impossible" lives on warm paper (ink/bone,
  evidence-plate collage with real objects casting shadows). On "*But* that morning" the ground
  turns dark (steel dawn → x-ray oxide-black) — ref 1's light→dark turn in meaning. In the
  monochrome x-ray world the **sky-blue drinking tube is the only blue** (the outside world /
  the will to live becomes the instrument of survival). Red appears only where stress, blood
  or the break is.
* **Type — the date split by the slit, and a drafting title block as the key take.** The opening
  date reads `THU ▕ MAY 1` with the sliver of dawn sky as its middle dot. "Mathematically precise
  mechanics of survival" is set as an engineering drawing's title block (`MECHANICS OF SURVIVAL ·
  DRAWN A. RALSTON · 05·01·2003`) — he is an engineer; his solution is a drawing.

## Colour
* Dawn steel: `ROCK.cold` walls, sky `#2E4A66 → #8FB3CB → peach #F2C9A0` rim glow, frost type.
* Paper plate: `C.paper` / `C.bone` ground, ink `#3A1C13` engraving, muted muscle `#B5655A`,
  red `#D2301E` only for the cut-ring, the blood curve and the shock floor.
* X-ray: `PAL.xray` (`#140E0C` ground, bone `#E9DFCC`, mid `#8C7B6A`), red only for stress / fracture,
  **sky blue only for the tube**.
* Monument: warm black `#16100D` with the gold-lit canyon (`C.gold`, `C.ochre`) inside the letters.

## Type
* `THU` / `MAY 1`, HOUR, `MINUTES`, counters: Big Shoulders Display 800–900.
* Labels (`RADIUS`, `ULNA`, `TORQUE`, `FULCRUM`, `SOFT TISSUE`, `CUT`, `BLOOD`, `SHOCK`): Martian Mono 600, ≥ 34 px.
* Equation `τ = r × F`: Fraunces italic (math italic voice) — equation as design.
* `ENDURANCE`: Unbounded 800 (the section's single hero statement); *the will to live*: Instrument Serif italic.
* Title block: Martian Mono + Fraunces title.
