# S7 · 885.26 → 1030.30 · "Rescue & The Lesson" — visual plan

> Owner: S7 agent. The film's resolution. Everything the viewer has carried for fifteen
> minutes — the blank note, INVULNERABLE, the cut blue threads, the vision of a son, the
> slit of sky, the chockstone — is paid off here, and the last frame is the simplest one.

## 1. Three decisions a template would never make (atelier rule)

* **Structural — "the search grid vs. the note."** The section opens on a paper search map
  where hundreds of sky-blue grid squares flip on: *nobody knows where to look.* It closes on
  one handwritten route sketch — a single dotted line — on a kitchen-table note: *they know
  exactly where to look.* The same dotted line is unfolded into the path world of the lesson
  (one curve, three views: note sketch → perspective path → ink). Mid-section, a pull-back
  turns the Leo moment into one tile among thousands of adventure snapshots, which flip into
  thousands of blank cards — the lesson the next scenes write.
* **Colour — sky blue escalates from a pinprick to the last word.** S6 handed us a phone
  whose signal is empty. S7 starts with a single sky-blue pulse on a bleached map, then
  grid → helicopter orbits → rescue livery → the threads of people → finally the only
  saturated colour on screen is the **blue ink** of the note in warm morning light.
  Warmth returns in parallel: bleached desert → clinical white → gold → winter → the
  golden park → a warm kitchen → warm near-black.
* **Type — handwriting is the film's last voice.** Reenie Beanie appears only on the note.
  Unbounded appears exactly once ("127 HOURS"). Words become terrain once: SKILLS /
  TECHNOLOGY / CONDITION are painted on the path in perspective, and the boulder lands on them.
